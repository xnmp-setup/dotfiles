Description

If you prefer a coffee table without corners and with enormous WOW factor, this replica designer Isamu Noguchi Coffee Table is your perfect match. A flawless organic design combining wood and glass invites beauty and class into any living room or reception area. Enjoy this unique coffee table in black, walnut or natural wood with glass top.

Designer: Isamu Noguchi

Style: Coffee Table

Colour: Natural, Walnut, Black

Material: 15mm Thick Tempered Clear Glass

Base Material: 30mm Solid Ash Wood

Assembly: Simple

Height: 40cm

Width: 125cm

Depth: 88cm

Warranty: 12 Months

Commercial Grade Quality
