Use case: ui-mockup
Create a polished straight-on 3440 by 1440 ultrawide Linux desktop theme concept mockup called Rapture Light — Lagoon. This is a visual preview for choosing colors before implementation. No monitor frame, no perspective tilt, no outside annotations, no branding watermark. Show an elegant minimal top status bar, a Ghostty terminal window on the left and a compact file explorer window on the right, with generous wallpaper visible between and above them. The windows are believable everyday desktop apps, flat opaque surfaces, subtle thin borders and modest soft shadows, restrained corners, legible typography and clear selected tabs. Do not make a dashboard, website landing page or futuristic HUD. Use tasteful normal desktop density and spacing. The terminal contains short readable shell lines about projects and git status; the file explorer contains a few realistic folders like Projects, Pictures and Notes. Keep the same fundamental layout for the alternative Reef mockup so differences are about color and atmosphere.
Theme direction: a light counterpart to Rapture's deep-navy and pastel cyan identity. Almost-white ice-blue backgrounds #f0f8fc, pale blue panels #e2f0f7, dark slate-blue text #20394c, readable marine-teal accent #267da6, soft selection #d0e8f3 and delicate blue-grey borders. The interface is airy, calm and cool, with a whisper of aqua. Tiny restrained coral, seafoam and violet syntax colors echo Rapture's original accent spectrum without becoming rainbow UI. No dark UI panels, extreme transparency, saturated full-window backgrounds or low-contrast grey text.
Wallpaper direction: a breathtaking sunlit tropical reef bursting with vitality, photographed underwater in exceptionally clear turquoise water. Healthy branching, plate and soft corals form a rich organic reef across the lower half, with vivid but natural coral-pink, warm orange, golden and violet details, a school of small orange anthias and a few jewel-blue fish moving through the water. Beautiful sunlight rays enter from the water surface above, with broad tranquil pale-aqua open water giving breathing room. This should feel like a living, thriving ecosystem with varied organisms, not a sterile perfect garden, fantasy aquarium or bleached reef. Rich color and detail in the coral stays in the wallpaper; the foreground apps remain quiet and readable. Use realistic photographic optics, believable coral textures and natural water depth. The overall mood is luminous, restorative, joyful and full of life.
The name Rapture Light — Lagoon may appear subtly in a window title. The result should convincingly communicate the theme as a coherent desktop, with the light-blue app palette and exuberant reef wallpaper clearly visible. No extra concept labels or color hex codes in the image.
