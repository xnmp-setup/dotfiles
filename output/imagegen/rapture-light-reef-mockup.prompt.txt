Use case: ui-mockup
Create a polished straight-on 3440 by 1440 ultrawide Linux desktop theme concept mockup called Rapture Light — Reef. This is a visual preview for choosing colors before implementation. No monitor frame, no perspective tilt, no outside annotations, no branding watermark. Show an elegant minimal top status bar, a Ghostty terminal window on the left and a compact file explorer window on the right, with generous wallpaper visible between and above them. The windows are believable everyday desktop apps, flat opaque surfaces, subtle thin borders and modest soft shadows, restrained corners, legible typography and clear selected tabs. Do not make a dashboard, website landing page or futuristic HUD. Use tasteful normal desktop density and spacing. The terminal contains short readable shell lines about projects and git status; the file explorer contains a few realistic folders like Projects, Pictures and Notes. Keep the same fundamental layout as the alternative Lagoon mockup so differences are about color and atmosphere.
Theme direction: a light counterpart to Rapture with noticeably sky-blue surfaces, still light and easy to read. Pale sky-blue backgrounds #e7f3fb, gently deeper blue panels #d8eaf5, dark ocean-slate text #263d57, readable marine-blue accent #326eaa, pale blue selection #c5e0f2 and fine blue-grey borders. The interface has more ocean-blue personality than a white theme, without becoming saturated or dark. Tiny restrained coral, emerald-teal and soft violet syntax colors echo Rapture's original accent spectrum without becoming rainbow UI. No dark UI panels, extreme transparency, saturated full-window backgrounds or low-contrast grey text.
Wallpaper direction: a lush tropical reef bursting with vitality under bright shallow-water daylight. A wide underwater vista with vivid naturally varied branching, plate and soft corals sweeping through the lower half; pink and orange corals, small violet fans, healthy golden-green textures, schools of brilliant orange anthias and jewel-blue reef fish. Clear turquoise and azure water, visible soft sunbeams and a shimmering sunlit surface; deeper cobalt-blue water in the distance adds depth without gloom. Keep broad open water around the reef and plenty of breathing room rather than filling every inch with fish or objects. The ecosystem looks healthy, varied, energetic and living, with authentic underwater photographic texture and organic irregularity, not a fantasy aquarium or a sterile perfect coral garden. Coral colors remain in the wallpaper while apps stay serene and readable. The overall mood is joyful, radiant and immersive, a flourishing tropical ocean rather than a muted underwater scene.
The name Rapture Light — Reef may appear subtly in a window title. The result should convincingly communicate the theme as a coherent desktop, with the light-blue app palette and exuberant reef wallpaper clearly visible. No extra concept labels or color hex codes in the image.
